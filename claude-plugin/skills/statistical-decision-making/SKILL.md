# Statistical decision making

Honor the role of each evidence source:

- Repair checks old feedback-bearing tasks. It may reject or create a challenger, never an
  active champion.
- Cache evidence may support repair screening, never a fail-to-pass label without a fresh
  champion control and never promotion.
- Validation uses exactly twelve fresh matched candidate/champion task pairs and is the only
  positive promotion evidence.
- Shadow certification is feedback-dark. Its disposition may update certification but must
  not produce a diagnostic hypothesis.
- Historical and baseline evidence is descriptive only on a protocol-compatible matched
  intersection.

Use the signed policy disposition and reported uncertainty. Do not compare raw scores from
different subsets, count repeated trials as independent tasks, pool incompatible protocol
hashes, impute missing baseline results, or promote an observed winner when the result is
inconclusive. Account for campaign-level repeated-testing budget and preregistered cost,
latency, capability, and stratum-regression vetoes.
